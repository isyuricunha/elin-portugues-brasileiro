Adventurer's Weekly No. 27: Taxes,Grandma Shu,10,1

<i>Welcome to Adventurer's Weekly, where I answer you youngins' questions on money.</i>


This week's letter is by a recent arrival from Zanan: <color=blue>"Grandma Shu, this is my first month in Olvina and I'm already being taxed! How is this fair, I don't have an oren to my name!"</color>

Okay, youngin'. First thing you do is take a deep breath and calm yerself.

Second thing you do is realize taxes ain't so bad.
{p}
Yes, I get it. Grandma Shu was once like you, grumblin' about paying my hard-earned orens to some royal I'd never see. But when you oughta realize is your first couple tax bills as an adventurer are about getting you to go out and <b>do</b> things — can't have a bunch of "adventurers" loafin' around, gettin' up to mischief. Without taxes, many youngins wouldn't have that push to explore the world and find ways to make money.

Your first couple bills should be easily covered by foragin' a few flowers, doing a few odd jobs in town. But this issue ain't about gettin' the money to pay yer taxes, it's about <b>understandin'</b> them.
{p}
<b>Tax Structure</b>

Forget property and income taxes — as an adventurer, you might find a fortune in a nefia one month an' go hungry the next. Hence, you're taxed as such under Mysilia's jurisdiction:

*A base value. This starts at 500 orens and increases by 500 annually, up to a total of 5,500 orens after 10 years.

*A fame value. This is 2 orens to 1 fame under 5000: advanced adventurers' bills still grow past that, but at a reduced rate.

*Voluntary tax. If you wanna get in good with Mysilia, you can sign up for these at a maid; get your landlord to appoint one if there ain't any. These go up to 20,000 every month, and you'll get a gold bar for every 1,000 orens you pay.
{p}
Your tax bill is all these added up; if you have trouble figurin' it out, you can always check the tax breakdown at yer home board. The biggest monthly bill them bastards can send you is 1,000,000 orens — as much as they'd like to, they can't milk you for more. I'm not sure why, and I honestly find that more disturbin'.


<b>Tax Evasion</b>

Unwilling to pay, are ya? Well, there's always Derphy. I don't do business there ever since fallin' out with Mistress Persephone, but hey, if you're as free-spirited as that lady you can be a criminal like her, too.
{p}
But if you oren-pinchin' youngins don't wanna run afoul of the law, ya can do public service instead. Fall behind on 3 bills an' each one after that'll cost you 40 karma. You just have to make that up every month, appease the taxman with sweat instead of coin. Clear out nefia and make the land safer, help out folks with their requests, that kinda thing. Your tax bills won't be overlooked, but at least you won't be a criminal.

Or if you don't mind payin' in another coin, I know a couple hillfolk out east who could help ya find "lost" bills for some small medals. Note that "paying" your taxes this way won't earn you gold bars if you signed up for voluntary tax — ya gotta pay the original bill for that.

There's always trying to fool tax assessors, but I think it's a waste of gold bars. Should you get really famous adventurin', youngin, taxes aren't really a problem.
{p}
<b>Automatic Taxation</b>

Most youngins know ya can ticket a tax box an' take it home. But if yer <b>really</b> lazy, there's always the salary transfer an' transfer tax payment policies. Once ya sign up for these at yer home board, yer bank account will handle both yer salary an' taxes; so long as the first exceeds the second, you'll never have to worry 'bout losing bills or missing payments ever again.

***

<i>As they say in Yerl: the only certain things in life are death and taxes. Both seem scary, but if you youngins take them in stride you'll soon realize there's no need to get upset about either.

Write me again anytime, youngins, and look forward to the next issue!</i>
