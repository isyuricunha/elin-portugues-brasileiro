Adventurer's Weekly No. 22: Marriage,Yowyn Correspondent Celia,10,1

<i>Hi! Welcome to Adventurer's Weekly, where this week I'm covering a topic near and dear to <b>my</b> heart.</i>


<b>Marriage!</b>

My boyfriend recently proposed to me, and Mr. Hans suggested I share the experience with you readers! Adventurers from all over Ylva are going to read about my wedding and learn what marriage in Tyris is like!

{p}
<b>Proposal</b>

It all started some weeks ago when he brought me an engagement ring and popped the question…! I'd have preferred an engagement amulet, but a ring is traditional and also really romantic!

We've been together for a while now, so of course I said yes! I hear adventurers tie the knot pretty quickly — trusting each other with your lives in a nefia really builds bonds — but we ordinary folk prefer to take things slow, help each other out and really get to know one another over time! There's no right or wrong, only that you really care about each other!

It's a great ring — just looking at it makes me feel all fuzzy and melty inside, no one but me is ever going to wear this gift from my beloved! That's what I should call him now, right? Hehe.
{p}
<b>Preparations</b>

Now that we're engaged, we're going to the scrivener today to buy a marriage permit! It costs quite a sum, but it covers booking the venue, the wedding buffet, guest invitations, all that stuff! Everything will be handled for you; all you have to do is show up on the big day!

Yes, some people prefer to save money and don't formalize their marriages. After all, if you act as if you're married, you may as well be. But at the same time, I want to make things official with my beloved, and I'm willing to pay for it!

Oh… they also sell divorce papers here. Ew, that's kind of cynical, isn't it? Well, it can't be helped. Now that we've got the permit, we can discuss actually holding the wedding!
{p}
<b>The Big Day</b>

It's finally here! The day of our wedding!

Look, Beloved! Everyone we know came — isn't it amazing to have all these people wish for our happiness? Ohh, all those bouquets must have cost a fortune!

The vow, the kiss, the cake-cutting with such an exquisite cutter… this is the happiest day of my life! My head's still spinny from it all… I'm seeing the world in a whole new light! They say you learn so much more when you have that special someone to make it worthwhile, hehe!

And with the ceremony over, it's time for the buffet… wait, Beloved. Who are those women barging into the church?
{p}
Kyaa! And why are they throwing flams at us?

W-what, they're your <i>wives</i>? You had a harem all this time? I don't care if it's okay in Eulderna, this is North Tyris!

<i>*Slap* *Sound of body hitting the ground*</i>

By all the Pillars, that was satisfying. Holy Father, how much do divorce papers cost?

<i>T-that</i> much? There's no way I can afford that, I'm going to be a pervert's wife for the rest of my life…

<b><i>…Fine!</i></b> If I can kill all my rivals, then my beloved will only have eyes for <b><i>me!</i></b>
