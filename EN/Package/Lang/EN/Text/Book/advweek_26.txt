Adventurer's Weekly No. 26: Delegated Farming,Yowyn Correspondent Celia,10,1

<i>Hi! Welcome to Adventurer's Weekly, where we cover topics near and dear to your brave hearts. Today, I'm introducing a helpful service from the Church of Kumiromi: delegated farming!</i>


Adventurers regularly leave home for weeks or months, and often don't have time to tend to their crops! Happily, the Church offers basic care for your fields, setting your heart at ease while you're away!


{p}
<b>Getting Started</b>

As it happens, I'm slated to head down to Olvina and pick up a new strain of grape seeds for my garden! However, the trip could take half a month, and by the time I return all my precious veggies will have withered! I don't have anyone around to help, so the harvest — and more importantly, my seed strains — will be lost!

Oh no! What do I do?

Thankfully, my hearth stone's level two, so I'm eligible to request help from the Church — all I have to do is activate the ordnance from my home board, and they'll dispatch volunteers to my rescue!
{p}
<b>Setting Up</b>

Before I leave, I need to ensure good working conditions for the church's helpers: farm signs to indicate which crops need minding, and proper shared storages to ensure the harvest is correctly stored. It's not just fruits and veggies — even by-products like sticks and straw need a place to go, and if there isn't one the volunteers will just place them in the shipping bin! Good if you want to sell them, bad if you want to cook with your veggies later!

While I'm away, the harvest will be brought in just before it withers — usually five days after ripening! What they <b>won't</b> do is water, not with so many adventurers needing their services, so I guess I'll just have to hope for rain! Or maybe not, since I'll be on the road…
{p}
Let's hope I didn't make any mistakes and they do their job right!


<b>Harvest</b>

Two weeks have passed, and now I'm back! Let's see… yep, the church volunteers have been through my gardens and harvested everything I marked with a farm sign! They've also not just replanted my veggies, but also improved the seeds — not as much as if I'd done it myself, but it's something!
{p}
Looking into the shared fridge and box, both my veggies and their by-products are neatly put away — minus went into replanting and payment for their work, of course! After everything, I'm left with about a third of the yield I would've had were I at home, compared to nothing at all and losing my veggie strains!

All in all, I'd say using delegated farming was a great success!
{p}
<b>Tips and Tricks</b>

* Delegated farming begins work at 6 am!

* If your plants leave remnants, you can bring in the harvest yourself and ask delegated farming to just replant your fields from the leftovers. Convenient if you don't want to bother with sickles!

* Different seed strains of the same crop in adjacent plots will be homogenized. Separate your delicate and crispy turnips if you want to keep them distinct!

* Seeds are improved from west to east, south to north. If you swap the north-easternmost seed with the south-westernmost, your crop quality might improve faster…

{p}
Thanks to tireless work from the Church of Kumiromi, adventurers can explore North Tyris without worrying about their crops at home! Even if you don't worship him, delegated farming's worth being grateful for, isn't it?

Thank you for reading, and we'll see you in next week's issue!
