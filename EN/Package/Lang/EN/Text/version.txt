{topic, Elin Update Information}
■ 11/22 2026 EA 23.362 Stable
(Translated by Kuppy)

After updating to a Stable version, there is a good chance that Mods made for previous versions may stop working or behave unexpectedly. If you encounter strange behavior, please try disabling your Mods first.

[Highlights]
* Updated the home quest. You can now play the continuation of the home story.
* Added a large number of Bunny, Nurse, Sister, and Police costume equipment.
* Added large furniture to decorate the entrances of your base, such as great doors, great door frames, castle gates, and drop gates.
* Party members can now also be dismissed through the "Actually..." dialogue option. Also added a "Release into the Wild" option when dismissing characters.
* The NPC "Young Lady" will now fuse, and the NPC "Young Ladymaton" has been added. NPCs with the "Stick Together!" feat will also fuse more actively.

[Content Additions]
* Added the non-random enchantment "Enhance Heal".
* Added the spell "Summon Putit".
* Added the head gear "Bunny Ears".
* Added the torso gear "Bunny Suit".
* Added the waist gear "Bunny Tail".
* Added the foot gear "Pair of Bunny Tights".
* Added the head gear "Nurse Cap".
* Added the torso gear "Nurse Tunic".
* Added the waist gear "Nurse Skirt".
* Added the arm gear "Pair of Nurse Gloves".
* Added the foot gear "Pair of Nurse Sandals".
* Added the head gear "Sister Cap".
* Added the torso gear "Sister Tunic".
* Added the waist gear "Sister Skirt".
* Added the foot gear "Pair of Sister Boots".
* Added the head gear "Police Cap".
* Added the torso gear "Police Tunic".
* Added the waist gear "Police Skirt".
* Added the red book "Weekly Adventurer Issue 26: Delegated Farming".
* Added the red book "Weekly Adventurer Issue 27: Taxes".
* Added the item "Grimoire of Forbidden Arts". Added it to the starting recipes.
* Added the tool "Pair of Handcuffs", along with its recipe.
* Added the junk "School Bag".
* Added a recipe for the furniture "Sturdy Treasure Chest". Also slightly expanded its container size.
* Added 2 types of the furniture "Great Door", along with their recipes.
* Added 5 types of the furniture "Great Door Frame", along with their recipes.
* Added the furniture "Castle Gate", along with its recipe.
* Added the furniture "Drop Gate", along with its recipe.
* Added the furniture "Foreign Alcove", along with its recipe.
* Added the furniture "Great Magic Circle".
* Added the furniture "Rack", along with one skin.
* Added the furniture "Pulpit", along with its recipe.
* Added the furniture "Security Camera", along with its recipe.
* Added the furniture "Huge Blackboard", along with one skin.
* Added the NPC "Young Ladymaton".
* Added calico and brown tabby skins for the NPC "Cat".
* Added a winter outfit for the NPC "Efrond".
* Added 2 new monsters.
* Added BGM for Fortune Bell.

[Additions and Changes]
* PNG images placed in the "Deco" folder of the replacement folder can now be used as deco decorations for widgets.
* Added a "Shuffle" toggle for the playback order to the BGM playlist editing screen of gramophones and the like.
* Added several pieces of art to the in-game art book. Also replaced older concept art with the versions updated in Elin State of the Game.
* Improved item highlighting in build mode.
* Doubled the vertical size of objs_L.
* When a block or object is copied with a fake block or fake object, the item's graphic will now reflect the graphic of what was copied.
* Relics can now be renamed.
* Status ailments, buffs, debuffs, etc. will now record a reference to the character that inflicted them.
* Divine equipment held by NPCs you have parted with or released into the wild will now be automatically sent to the PC's inventory.
* While affected by the status "Poison", the amount of HP recovered will now be halved.
* Changed the level of the "Devout" feat of the class "Priest" from 1 to 2.
* The feat "Musician" can no longer be inherited through genes.
* Songs will now be learned as the skill level of the skill "Music" increases (songs and such are planned to be added in the future).
* NPCs other than the PC and allies can now also use the spell "Resurrection".
* When a Scroll of Flying is used on equipment, the amount of decrease and increase in its stats will now be equal. Also, the enhancement level will no longer affect the amount by which stats are changed by a Scroll of Flying.
* The NPC "Samurai" will now equip a katana. Also added dialogue lines.
* Added various Bunny equipment to the rare drops of the NPC "Bunny".
* Added Nurse equipment to the rare drops of the NPCs "Pink Nurse", "What Once Was Nurse", and "Nurse".
* Added Sister equipment to the rare drops of the NPCs "Mother" and "Nun".
* Added Police equipment to the rare drops of the NPC "Hot Guy".
* Added the Fortune Bell BGM to the casino prizes.
* Updated the Chinese translation.

[Mod Changes] (By DK)
* Mods can now add custom Deco features.
* Enhanced support for creating and registering custom widgets in Mods.
* Updated the Modding Kit so that it can also handle types partially loaded by Mods.
* Improved the SpriteReplacer API and its usability.
* Added support for standing portrait-style images in dialogue windows and other interfaces.
* Improved error handling in the Modding Kit and made improvements to the Mod Viewer.
* Fixed a bug related to retrieving Mod query results.
* Fixed an issue where IO.IsCompressed incorrectly detected JSON files with a UTF-8 BOM as compressed files.

[Fixes]
* Fixed an issue where, when decorating widgets with decos, a deco could be displayed in front of the widget even when the "Display Behind Widget" option was enabled.
* Fixed an error that rarely occurred when crafting.
* Fixed an issue where, after escorting an NPC in an escort request, the NPC would die after a while instead of disappearing.
* Fixed an issue where bomb rocks and the like could be detonated by their own explosion after dying.
* Fixed an issue where, when attaching save data to feedback, the save currently being played was sent regardless of the selection in the dropdown.
* Fixed an issue where the Wide Area enchantment of scrubbers sold by ravian wandering merchants was limited to Lv1.
* Fixed an error that occurred when a follower hung in the wilds was recruited by brushing and remained hung.
* Fixed an issue where a pet's fur could not be sheared with the area effect of a pair of shears when the pet was on an item that blocks movement, such as a straw bale.
* Fixed an error that could occur when tracking a quest from the journal while the quest tracker widget was hidden.
* Fixed an issue where speed was sometimes not recalculated when moving from water to land on the turn immediately after entering a new map.
* Fixed an overflow issue related to tourism value.
* Fixed an issue where resetting feats with an elixir of Oblivion would also reset feats obtained from race or class (such as Gourmet, which the PC can obtain with FP). The fix will also be applied when loading a game if these feats have already been reset.
* Fixed an issue where using "Mischief" learned from an Embryolith on yourself would cause a null reference error.
* Fixed an error that occurred when a younger cat sister was fused.
* Other minor fixes and adjustments.
