## Rulings pending kickoff review

- ORDINANCE → "Decreto" (never "portaria": that is government paperwork
  jargon, not fantasy flavor). Applies everywhere the EN says ordinance.
- BR-GRAMMAR: NEVER drop articles and prepositions: "o quanto quiser"
  (not "quanto quiser"), "de/da/do" in every possessive chain. Small
  function words are the difference between natural PT and machine-ese.

- SOURCE OF TRUTH: the EN column. Translate EN-to-PT. JP is auxiliary
  only, for disambiguation. Where the EN column itself carries CJK text
  (backer credits left verbatim by the official EN release), the PT
  mirrors the EN release verbatim: do NOT translate from the JP column.

- God names: keep original (machine/wind/... are god domains, keep as
  proper nouns, lowercase only in generic use).
- Character names: keep as-is across all sheets.
